About Me

Welcome to Jianxi Su's personal website (always under construction)!

  • I am an Associate Professor at the Department of Insurance, Legal Studies and Real Estate at the University of Georgia.

  • Before UGA, I was an Associate Professor at Purdue University, I served as the Director of the Actuarial Science Program at Purdue.

  • Prior to joining Purdue in 2016, I completed my PhD at York University under the supervision of Edward Furman (2012-2015).

  • During my PhD candidature, I was a research associate at the Model Validation and ORSA Implementation team of Sun Life Financial (Toronto).

  • I held a Post-doctoral position at the Schulich School of Business for six months in 2016.

  • I obtained the Associate of the Canadian Institute of Actuaries (ACIA) in 2015 and the Fellow of the Society of Actuaries (FSA) in 2017.

  • In April 2022, I received the tenure, which would not have been possible without the constant and unconditional supports from my family, collaborators and colleagues.

  • My research expertise revolves around statistics modeling, particularly in the context of actuarial pricing and quantitative risk management.
